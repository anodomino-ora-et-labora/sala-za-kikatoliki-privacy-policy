Privacy & Data Commitment

Revised: February 2026

I. Our Commitment

Your spiritual journey is personal, and we believe your data should stay that way. This document outlines how Sala Za Kikatoliki operates to ensure your peace of mind while using our application.

II. Data Transparency

We do not operate central databases or tracking servers. The application is built to be self-contained. The only piece of information the app interacts with is:

  • Display Name: A name you provide to customize the interface. This stays strictly on your mobile hardware.

III. Third-Party Interactions

We do not sell, trade, or leak your information. Since no data is sent to our servers, there is no digital trail shared with third-party advertisers or analytics firms.

IV. User Control

You have absolute sovereignty over your data. Because your name is stored in the app's local cache, removing the app or clearing the app storage via your phone's settings will permanently erase that data from existence.

V. Safe for Families

Our app is built for a multi-generational audience. We comply with family-friendly standards, ensuring that younger users can explore their faith in a secure environment without hidden tracking.
